Three states of config.txt, read off the card before it was overwritten
Saturday 24 October 2026, from the project 5 card in the reader on skyhorizon


WHAT THIS IS, AND WHY IT IS HERE RATHER THAN LOST

The card holding project 5's bench-adxl345 image was about to be
overwritten with project 10's bench-iio-image. Its boot partition carried
four files with real timestamps rather than the frozen Apr 6 2011 epoch
that every file written by the wic image carries, which is what marks them
as hand edits made after flashing:

    wifi.conf                 Tuesday 21 October 2026, 09:53
    config.txt.i2c-backup     Tuesday 21 October 2026, 10:47
    config.txt.bak            Tuesday 21 October 2026, 11:23
    config.txt                Tuesday 21 October 2026, 11:46

The image itself is archived several times over, most recently as
proj05-bench-adxl345/2026-10-21_697ee2b, so nothing about the build was at
risk. These four files were in no archive at all. A card archive would
have held them and none was taken of this card.

wifi.conf was deliberately NOT copied. It holds the SSID and the PSK, it
belongs in ~/bench/credentials where scripts/flash.sh reads it from, and
nothing in this repository ever holds one.


THE SEQUENCE, WHICH IS THE WHOLE CONTENT

All three config.txt files are 2738 or 2742 bytes and differ from each
other on line 252 and nowhere else:

    diff config.txt.i2c-backup config.txt.bak
    252c252
    < dtoverlay=bench-adxl345
    ---
    > dtoverlay=bench-adxl345-spi

    diff config.txt.bak config.txt
    252c252
    < dtoverlay=bench-adxl345-spi
    ---
    > dtoverlay=bench-adxl345

So, in time order:

    10:47   dtoverlay=bench-adxl345        the I2C arm
    11:23   dtoverlay=bench-adxl345-spi    the SPI arm
    11:46   dtoverlay=bench-adxl345        back to the I2C arm

Both arms were selected on the card that morning, 36 minutes apart, and
the card was left on I2C.


WHAT IT CORROBORATES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

It corroborates that the SPI arm was actually exercised on Tuesday 21
October 2026 and not merely considered, which matters because that is the
session where the SPI arm read DEVID as 0x00 and where the cause of both
arms' silence was found to be the supply: the module's VCC and GND were
one net at ground, fed from header pin 9. See supply-fault-2026-10-21.csv
and its .md.

It does NOT show which arm was active during any particular reading. A
config.txt line selects an overlay for the NEXT boot, so these timestamps
are edit times, not boot times, and nothing here records when the board
was power cycled between them. Whether a given scan in that session ran
under the I2C or the SPI overlay has to come from that session's own
record and not from this file.

It also does not show what the overlay did, only which one was asked for.
The four bench-adxl345*.dtbo files were all present in overlays/ on the
card throughout.


HOW IT WAS OBTAINED

    sudo mount -o ro /dev/sde1 /mnt/card
    cp /mnt/card/config.txt /mnt/card/config.txt.bak \
       /mnt/card/config.txt.i2c-backup ~/cardcfg/
    sudo umount /mnt/card

Read only, nothing written to the card. The copies retain no useful
timestamps of their own, because cp without -p stamps them with the time
of the copy; the times above were read from the ls -la of the mounted boot
partition while it was still untouched, and that listing is the only place
they existed.

That is the reason this file records them as text rather than keeping the
three files: their contents differ by one line, their value was in their
modification times, and a copy does not carry those.
