The SEN0032 with its harness measured good, Saturday 24 October 2026
===================================================================

This closes the thread opened by supply-fault-2026-10-21. That day the
module's VCC and GND were one net and it took five hours to find. This
took about ten minutes, because the harness was measured BEFORE the
scan rather than theorised about after it.

WHAT WAS MEASURED FIRST, probe from GPIO25 on header pin 22, both
biases at each point, per walkthrough/08-board-bringup.

    probe in mid air          pull-up 1  pull-down 0   floating, so the
                                                       instrument works
    module's own VCC row      pull-up 1  pull-down 1   driven high
    module's own GND row      pull-up 0  pull-down 0   driven low

Different answers at the two rows, which is the whole point: on Tuesday
both rows would have read 0 0. VCC came straight from header pin 1 and
GND straight from header pin 9, neither through a breadboard rail.

WIRING, as on Friday 9 October 2026 when this part last answered

    VCC  pin 1      GND  pin 9      SDA  pin 3      SCL  pin 5
    CS   pin 17 (3V3, I2C mode)     SDO  pin 6 (GND, address 0x53)

THE RESULT

    root@raspberrypi3-64:/# i2cdetect -y 1
    ... every cell --, no address anywhere on the bus ...

    [7.131172] bench-adxl345-i2c 1-0053: error -EREMOTEIO: cannot read DEVID
    [7.140159] bench-adxl345-i2c: probe of 1-0053 failed with error -121

The sixth -121. Completely empty, which is an electrically absent part
rather than a clamping one.

WHY THIS ONE IS CONCLUSIVE AND THE EARLIER ONES WERE NOT

Every external variable is eliminated by measurement rather than by
argument:

  - the supply and the return are present at the module's own rows, and
    are not the same net
  - header pins 1, 9, 3 and 5 carried an X-NUCLEO-IKS4A1 twenty minutes
    earlier, which answered on six addresses and identified both of its
    IMUs by WHO_AM_I. See projects/10-iio-iks4a1/docs/evidence/
    i2c-scan-2026-10-24.txt. That is a positive control on the same four
    pins, which this bench has never had before
  - the I2C controller produced a full scan table rather than an error,
    and GPIO2 and GPIO3 read 3.3 V under both biases on Tuesday
  - the CS and SDO straps are the arrangement that read DEVID 0xe5 on
    Friday 9 October 2026

What remains is the male pin against the plated hole wall, six of them,
and no instrument outside the module can reach one. The inventory has
said since Tuesday 6 October 2026 that this is a wiring and contact
question and never a dead-part question, because the part has been read
successfully before.

WHAT IT COSTS AND WHAT UNBLOCKS IT

Decision 129 chose to replace the wiring rather than retry it, and its
remedy is a soldering sitting. Decision 135 reopened it because there is
no iron here. This entry does not reopen anything further: it removes
the alternatives. A soldering iron and one 8-pin 2.54 mm male header is
the only route to this part.

Project 5 no longer depends on it: decision 136 moves that driver to the
IKS4A1's IMU. Project 11 does, and stays, because two of its six
criteria are already met against this part and its library, packaging
and symbols file are an ADXL345 library. Criteria 1, 3 and 6 are its
only open ones and all three need this part to answer.

So the iron is no longer a way to rescue a dead sensor. It is what
stands between project 11 and its acceptance, and it also restores the
two-bus seam project 5 loses on the IKS4A1, which carries SPI only for a
DIL24 sensor.
