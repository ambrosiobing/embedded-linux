LEAF SYMBOLS AGAINST CALLCHAIN ENTRIES, which is the distinction that
reconciles two readings of the same data.

root@raspberrypi3-64:/tmp# perf script -i lock.data | grep -cE "fault_lock|__delay"
3
root@raspberrypi3-64:/tmp# perf script -i lock.data | grep -c "cycles:P"
4000
root@raspberrypi3-64:/tmp# perf script -i lock.data -F cpu,time,sym | grep -E "fault_lock|__delay"
root@raspberrypi3-64:/tmp#

Three mentions in 4000 samples, and ZERO when only each sample's leaf
symbol is printed. So no sample's program counter lies inside the fault.
The three are callchain entries: samples taken at the moment the
interrupt mask lifts, whose stack passes through fault_lock, which is
also why _raw_spin_unlock_irqrestore tops the profile.

THE MECHANISM, MEASURED. Largest gap between consecutive samples, per CPU:

root@raspberrypi3-64:/tmp# cd /tmp && perf script -i lock.data -F cpu,time 2>/dev/null | awk '{c=$1; t=$2+0; if (p[c]!="") {d=t-p[c]; if (d>m[c]) m[c]=d} p[c]=t} END{for (k in m) printf "%s largest gap %.3f s\n", k, m[k]}'
[000] largest gap 0.024 s
[001] largest gap 0.028 s
[002] largest gap 0.036 s
[003] largest gap 3.004 s

Sampling on CPU 3 stopped for 3.004 s while the other three carried on at
a few tens of milliseconds. On a Cortex-A53 the PMU interrupt is an
ordinary interrupt rather than an NMI, fault_lock holds
spin_lock_irqsave for the whole window, and an interrupt that cannot be
taken cannot carry a sample. The fault disables the mechanism the
instrument needs in order to observe it.

WHAT THE CRITERION ASKED AND WHY IT CANNOT BE MET. It asks that perf
report attribute most lock-fault samples to fault_lock or __delay. Zero
samples can be attributed there, because none can be taken. The criterion
is not defective in its intent, it is unreachable on this architecture
for this fault, and the thing it was reaching for is better shown by the
3.004 s gap above.

A MEASUREMENT ERROR OF MINE, recorded rather than cleaned up. The first
recording used perf record -a across all four CPUs, three of which were
idle, so default_idle_call holds 9.24 per cent and idle entries litter
the listing. The intended fix was to pin the trigger with taskset and
record one CPU, and taskset is not on this image, joining timeout, ip -br
and partprobe among the BusyBox gaps. The per-CPU gap analysis above
answered the question better than the pinned recording would have, since
restricting the CPUs cannot manufacture samples that were never
delivered.
