THE ONE THING CHANGED, and the second attempt

The irqsoff tracer does function tracing underneath by default. A three
second window at that granularity overruns the per-CPU buffer, so the
longest window it managed to keep was the 2 ms one above. options
/function-trace turns the function tracing off and leaves the latency
measurement intact, which is what that knob is for. Nothing else
differed.
