THREE THINGS THAT QUALIFY THESE ROWS
====================================

1.  The board ran a hand-patched rt-capture that does not match the
    flashed image.

    The shipped rt-capture ran at ordinary priority against stress-ng on
    the same three cores, and its ring buffer overran twice, at 1480000
    and at 120000 samples. Rows 2 onward were taken after adding

        os.sched_setscheduler(0, os.SCHED_FIFO, os.sched_param(60))

    before a_in_scan_start, applied on the board with the original kept
    at /usr/bin/rt-capture.orig. Row 1 predates the patch and did not
    need it, having no load.

2.  The control kernel is not one symbol away from the real-time one.
    It is the whole fragment away.

    kas/bench-rt-generic.yml sets BENCH_RT_KERNEL = "0", and the bbappend
    adds rt.cfg only when that is "1", so the generic build carries none
    of EXPERT, PREEMPT_RT, IRQ_FORCED_THREADING, HIGH_RES_TIMERS,
    NO_HZ_FULL, CPU_ISOLATION, RCU_NOCB_CPU or
    CPU_FREQ_DEFAULT_GOV_PERFORMANCE.

    These rows are therefore measurements of the stock Raspberry Pi
    kernel, which is a real and useful baseline, and not of a control
    that differs from the experiment in one variable. Journal 57.

3.  The isolated rows carry isolcpus only.

    nohz_full=3 and rcu_nocbs=3 were placed in cmdline.txt and rejected
    by the kernel, which said so on the console and nowhere else:

        Housekeeping: nohz unsupported. Build with CONFIG_NO_HZ_FULL
        Unknown kernel command line parameters "nohz_full=3 rcu_nocbs=3"

    So isolated=yes in these rows means the scheduler was kept off CPU 3.
    The timer tick and that core's own RCU callbacks remained.

COLUMNS TO READ WITH CARE
=========================

ext_max_us      Untrustworthy in any row that lost edges. A dropped edge
                merges two periods and manufactures a deviation nothing
                experienced. Compare ext_edges against 30000 first: the
                idle rows lose 0 and 1, the loaded rows lose 6 to 1034,
                and the disagreement with int_max_us tracks that exactly.

ext_sd_us       Sound, but floored by quantisation. subsample_fraction is
                about 0.15, so the per-edge resolution is one sample
                period, 10 us, contributing roughly 2.9 us on its own.
                The write-path figure of 2.212 us in the idle tuned row
                sits at or below that floor and is not a measurement.

ext_ppm         Not a clock measurement. It is derived from the mean
                period, and a run that loses a contiguous block of edges
                reports a mean over a shortened span. It flipped sign
                between two comparable rows three minutes apart.

cyc_max_us      Trustworthy. Read from cyclictest's own
                "# Max Latencies:" line, which counts every sample. Note
                that the console summary printed beside it derives from
                the -h 400 histogram and saturates near 392.5, so the two
                disagree above 400 us and the column is the honest half.

governor        Says ondemand in the force_turbo row. That is the
                column's fault, not the board's: force_turbo pinned
                scaling_min_freq and scaling_max_freq both to 1500000, so
                cpufreq had one frequency to choose from while still
                calling itself ondemand.

CONDITIONS
==========

Network:   wlan0 on a phone hotspot, 192.168.92.85. Time sync active, so
           the Pi's clock was being disciplined during runs.
Thermal:   no throttling in any row, throttled_before and throttled_after
           both 0x0 throughout. Idle around 52 C, peaking at 65.7 C
           during the loaded rows.
Monitor:   rows 5 to 8 were taken with a two second temperature, throttle
           and clock monitor running on the serial console. Rows 1 to 4
           were not. Small, but it is a difference.
Reboots:   rows 1 to 4 on one boot, rows 5 to 7 on a second after adding
           isolcpus, row 8 on a third after adding force_turbo. KASLR is
           enabled, so kernel layout differs per boot and that variation
           is inseparable from isolation in this design. The fix is to
           reboot before every row, and it is on the list.
