        scarthgap, DISTRO_VERSION 5.0.20, MACHINE raspberrypi4-64
When:   16 September 2026, 06:56
Proves: Acceptance criterion 7, and it proves it before the kernel was
        compiled rather than after.

This is the check the whole kernel side of Project 8 exists to pass.
CONFIG_PREEMPT_RT depends on EXPERT && ARCH_SUPPORTS_RT, and arch/arm64
gained that select in 6.12 while meta-raspberrypi scarthgap still defaults
to 6.6. On 6.6 the option is silently dropped and the board boots a kernel
that is not preemptible while the results table claims otherwise.

Three facts had to line up, and each was checked separately:

  1. the version pin took            head -4 of the kernel's own Makefile
  2. every fragment line is a symbol  ./go ksym -f rt
  3. every line reached the .config   ./go kconfig -f rt, below

Step 1, from the unpacked source at
~/bench/build/tmp/work-shared/raspberrypi4-64/kernel-source/Makefile:

    # SPDX-License-Identifier: GPL-2.0
    VERSION = 6
    PATCHLEVEL = 12
    SUBLEVEL = 93

Step 2 indexed 21484 declarations from that tree and found all 31 fragment
symbols real, with two reported as consequences rather than requests:
IRQ_FORCED_THREADING, promptless and selected by arch/arm64/Kconfig,
arch/loongarch/Kconfig and arch/riscv/Kconfig; and LOCKUP_DETECTOR,
promptless and selected by lib/Kconfig.debug.

Step 3 is below. The .config was produced by

    bitbake -c kernel_configme virtual/kernel

which runs fetch, checkout and patch and then merges the fragments, so it
costs minutes rather than the hour a full kernel compile takes. The path is
given explicitly here because the automatic search was picking the wrong
kernel at the time; see journal entry 25.

What this does not prove:

  - that the kernel compiles, boots, or schedules anything on time
  - that /sys/kernel/realtime reads 1 on the board, which is criterion 1
  - any latency number

It proves that the configuration the board will be given is the
configuration that was asked for. Everything after this is the build, the
card and the wire.
