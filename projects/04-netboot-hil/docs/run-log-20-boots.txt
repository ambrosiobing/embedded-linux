Twenty consecutive automated boots.

Not yet run. This file is the shape of the evidence rather than the
evidence, and it is committed empty on purpose: the acceptance criterion
is twenty boots without manual intervention, and a file that guessed at
the numbers would be the one thing in this project a reader could catch.

How to produce it, once the lab is standing:

    cd projects/04-netboot-hil/runner
    for i in $(seq 1 20); do
        echo "=== run $i  $(date -Is)"
        pytest -q || echo "=== run $i FAILED"
    done 2>&1 | tee ../docs/run-log-20-boots.txt

What has to be recorded alongside the pass or fail, because it is the
project's own stated limitation:

  - how many of the twenty needed the plug pulled by hand. The bench has
    no relay, no switched USB hub and no smart plug, so a DUT wedged badly
    enough not to answer its own console is the one manual step in the
    loop. conftest.py prints a line asking for it rather than hiding it.
  - the wall-clock time of a run. The criterion is under three minutes
    including the soft reboot.
