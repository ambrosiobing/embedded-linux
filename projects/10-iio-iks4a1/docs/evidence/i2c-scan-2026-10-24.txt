X-NUCLEO-IKS4A1 on the Raspberry Pi 3B+, Saturday 24 October 2026
=================================================================

The first scan of this shield against a prediction rather than against
an expectation. Every address below was written down before the board
was powered, from UM3239 Rev 5 Table 1, and every WHO_AM_I value from
ST's own register headers read on Wednesday 7 October 2026. The two
documents had never been checked against each other.

WIRING, five leads, shield to Pi header. UM3239 Table 4 for the shield
side. Every contact is a Dupont pin in a soldered socket, which is why
no continuity preliminaries were run: see walkthrough/08-board-bringup
for the rule and what it exempts.

    CN6 pin 4   3.3 V              Pi pin 1
    CN5 pin 7   GND                Pi pin 9
    CN5 pin 9   I2C SDA            Pi pin 3, GPIO2
    CN5 pin 10  I2C SCL            Pi pin 5, GPIO3
    CN9 pin 6   LSM6DSV16X INT1    Pi pin 18, GPIO24

The interrupt lead was first put on pin 16, GPIO23, and moved to pin 18
to match bench-iks4a1-overlay.dts, which asks for <24 4>.

The Nucleo was not involved. The shield was read both stacked on an
unpowered NUCLEO-H7A3ZI-Q and lifted off it, with identical results,
and it should be left off: feeding 3.3 V into the connector back-powers
the Nucleo through its own rail.

PREDICTED versus SEEN

    0x19  LIS2DUXS12    ABSENT
    0x1e  LIS2MDL       seen
    0x38  STTS22H       seen
    0x44  SHT40AD1B     seen
    0x5d  LPS22DF       seen
    0x6a  an IMU        seen
    0x6b  the other     seen

Six of seven. Neither 0x18 nor 0x19 answered, so the LIS2DUXS12 is not
on the bus at all rather than at an unexpected address. UM3239 Table 3
gives its SDA and SCL as SB22 and SB16, and the Mode 3 block on page 6
lists SB22, SB19 and SB20 among bridges that are not mounted. Reaching
it would mean soldering, which this bench cannot do. It costs nothing
here: iio.cfg configures the IMU, the magnetometer, the pressure sensor
and the humidity sensor, and not that part.

THE IDENTIFICATION, which is the test that could have failed

    root@raspberrypi3-64:/# printf '0x6a: '; i2cget -y 1 0x6a 0x0f
    0x6a: 0x22
    root@raspberrypi3-64:/# printf '0x6b: '; i2cget -y 1 0x6b 0x0f
    0x6b: 0x70

    0x6a  WHO_AM_I 0x22  ->  LSM6DSO16IS
    0x6b  WHO_AM_I 0x70  ->  LSM6DSV16X

Read twice, identical. This settles the question docs/hardware.md raised
on Wednesday 7 October 2026 and said was not knowable from the register
headers: which IMU is strapped to which address. UM3239 Table 1 predicted
it, D5h for the LSM6DSO16IS and D7h for the LSM6DSV16X, and the board
agrees. bench-iks4a1-overlay.dts already declares both at exactly these
addresses, so no overlay change is needed.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW

No driver is bound. This card carries the project 5 image, which installs
the ADXL345 modules and names no kernel-module-st-lsm6dsx package, so
st_lsm6dsx is compiled, cached and absent from the rootfs. Nothing here
says the driver works, that the interrupt line is correct, or that any
sensor produces a sample. The scan and the identification are the bus
answering, nothing more.

The -121 line for 1-0053 in dmesg on this boot is the project 5 overlay
looking for an ADXL345 that is no longer wired. Expected, not a fault.
