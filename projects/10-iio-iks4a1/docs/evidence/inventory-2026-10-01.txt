WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
---------------------

Seven parts, seven verdicts, and every verdict is the one the earlier
measurements predicted:

  working             one part, the SHT40AD1B, through hwmon
  no-driver-in-image  four parts, every IIO device on the shield
  unsupported         two parts, recorded on purpose

The "no-driver-in-image" verdict is the finding of Wednesday 30 September
2026 arriving through the program written to detect it. Stock Raspberry Pi
OS carries none of the ST IIO drivers, and this is the inventory saying so
in the program's own words rather than in a journal entry.

AND ONE SCAN NOW ENUMERATES THE WHOLE BOARD, which no scan did before.
Earlier the two probe modes disagreed: the default saw 0x44 and missed
0x19, and "-r" did the reverse. Here the default scan shows all seven,
because 0x44 no longer needs probing at all. It reads UU, meaning the
sht4x driver has claimed it, and a claimed address is reported without
being probed.

So binding a driver to the part that hides under one probe mode is what
made a single scan complete. The UU that reads like an error to anyone
meeting it for the first time is the reason this grid is right.

STILL OPEN AT THIS ADDRESS
--------------------------

0x6a is listed with st_lsm6dsx on the strength of the part being an LSM6
family device. Whether that driver carries a compatible for the
LSM6DSO16IS specifically has not been checked in any kernel, and the note
in the row says so. It matters only once a driver exists to check.
