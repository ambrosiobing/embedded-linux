First bus scan of the X-NUCLEO-IKS4A1 wired to a Raspberry Pi 3B+.
Wednesday 30 September 2026.

Host:    Raspberry Pi 3B+, hostname eplepi
Kernel:  6.18.50+rpt-rpi-v8 aarch64, Raspberry Pi OS (Debian trixie)
Bus:     i2c-1, header pins 3 (GPIO2, SDA1) and 5 (GPIO3, SCL1)
Wiring:  shield CN6 pin 4 to header pin 1 (3V3)
         shield CN6 pin 6 or 7 to header pin 6 (GND)
         shield CN5 pin 9  (D14) to header pin 3
         shield CN5 pin 10 (D15) to header pin 5
         shield INT1 to header pin 18 (GPIO24), claimed CN9 pin 6 (D5),
         not used by this scan and not yet verified by continuity

Enabled with dtparam=i2c_arm=on in /boot/firmware/config.txt line 55,
then modprobe i2c-dev. The parameter alone is not enough: it enables the
controller, and the module is what creates /dev/i2c-1.

Six devices answered.

0x1e  LIS2MDL      magnetometer
0x38  STTS22H      temperature
0x44  SHT40        humidity and temperature

Not present: nothing answered at 0x18 or 0x19, where a LIS2DUXS12 would
be expected.

Identity registers, same session, same board.

root@eplepi:~# i2cget -y 1 0x6a 0x0f
0x22
root@eplepi:~# i2cget -y 1 0x6b 0x0f
0x70

Register 0x0f is WHO_AM_I across the ST LSM6 family.

0x22 is the value reported by the LSM6DSO16IS, the part carrying the
in-sensor processing unit. 0x70 is the value reported by the LSM6DSV16X,
the part carrying sensor fusion.

So on this shield:

  0x6a  LSM6DSO16IS   ISPU part
  0x6b  LSM6DSV16X    fusion part, and the one whose INT1 is claimed to
                      reach CN9 pin 6

This is the reverse of what bench-iks4a1-overlay.dts assumes, which binds
st,lsm6dsv16x at 0x6a. The driver checks WHO_AM_I during probe, so that
node would have found 0x22 where it expected 0x70 and refused to bind,
with the part appearing on the bus and absent from IIO.

The mapping above is read from two registers and from the shield's known
population. The authoritative confirmation is the driver's own probe once
the corrected overlay is applied: the name file under the registered IIO
device states which part the kernel matched.

Second and third scans, same session, nothing changed on the bench.

Second scan gained a seventh address at 0x19:

10: -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- 19 -- -- -- -- 1e --

Third scan lost it again:

10: -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- 1e --

The other six answered on all three. So the LIS2DUXS12 at 0x19 is
intermittent under i2cdetect, present on one scan in three, and the other
six are stable.

Checked against UM3239 Rev 5 Table 1, every address is this board's
default solder-bridge setting:

  0x19  LIS2DUXS12    SB20, ADD 33h
  0x1e  LIS2MDL       ADD 3Ch
  0x38  STTS22H       ADD 71h
  0x44  SHT40AD1B     ADD 89h
  0x5d  LPS22DF       SB29, ADD BBh
  0x6a  LSM6DSO16IS   SB35, ADD D5h
  0x6b  LSM6DSV16X    SB15, ADD D7h

DB5091 Rev 4 lists exactly these seven sensors on the main board of the
kit, the X-NUCLEO-IQS4A1. The detachable board on top, the
STEVAL-MKE001A1, carries the Qvar swipe electrodes and puts nothing on
this bus. The DIL24 socket is empty.

UM3239 Rev 5 Table 4, Arduino R3 UNO connectors, confirms the wiring:
